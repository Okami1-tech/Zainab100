Something I want you to know

My letter to you.

My Sarauniya, my Queen, my Hayati… 💜

Sometimes I wonder how something as ordinary as being course mates could become such a beautiful part of my life. We started with the simplest things—notes, assignments, little conversations—and somehow you became someone I hold so dearly in my heart.

I still remember asking you for notes because I hadn't copied mine properly. 😂 Who would have thought that such a small thing could become part of a story I would treasure?

I remember when your bag was stolen and I wanted to help calm you down. I couldn't make everything disappear, but I wanted you to remember that Allah knows best, even when we don't understand what has happened.

I notice the little things about you. How you encourage me to study, even when STA121 tries to humble me. 😂 How you remind me to eat, rest, and take care of myself. How you believe in me and sometimes motivate me without even realizing how much your words mean.

I appreciate you more than I always know how to express. I don't want my love to be only sweet words. I want it to show in my patience, my honesty, my effort, my respect, and the way I treat you when life is not easy.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. I am still growing, still learning, still working towards the man I want to become. But I pray that I keep becoming someone who brings you peace rather than confusion, reassurance rather than fear, and kindness rather than unnecessary pain.

And if Allah wills it for us, I pray that one day we will look back at these little beginnings from a home of our own. A home filled with sakinah, laughter, understanding, mercy, and barakah. A home where we remind each other of Allah and make du'a for one another even when the other person doesn't know.

I pray for a love that helps us become better people. A love that does not make us forget our Creator, but brings us closer to Him. A love with patience, good character, sincere effort, and the intention to do things the right way.

You don't have to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You don't have to be strong every single day. You can have difficult days, quiet days, days when you need rest, and days when you need someone to remind you that you matter.

I hope I always remember to listen to you, to understand you, and to make room for your feelings. I hope we keep learning how to communicate with gentleness, especially when we misunderstand each other.

And my love, please never forget to take care of yourself. Your health matters. Your peace matters. Your dreams matter. You matter beyond what you do for anyone else.

If there is one thing I want you to feel when you read this, it is that you are appreciated. Not only on special days. Not only when everything is going well. But in the ordinary moments too.

May Allah protect the good in you, ease what feels heavy, bless your efforts, grant you beneficial knowledge, and place light in your path. May He grant you happiness that brings you closer to Him and keep you safe from what harms you.

I don't know every detail of what the future holds. But I know that I want to keep growing, working, praying, and doing my best. And if Allah writes our future together, I pray that we meet that future with grateful hearts.

Until then, let's keep choosing kindness. Let's keep encouraging each other. Let's keep making du'a. Let's keep building something beautiful with sincerity and patience, one day at a time.

You are precious to me, Sarauniya. And I hope my actions continue to remind you of that, long after you've finished reading these words.

With love and du'a,
Muhammad 💜
