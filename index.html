My Sarauniya 💜

My Queen, my Hayati…

Sometimes I wonder how something as ordinary as being course mates could become such a beautiful part of my life. We started with the simplest things—notes, assignments, and little conversations—and somehow you became someone I hold so dearly in my heart.

I still remember asking you for notes because I hadn't copied mine properly. 😂 Who would have thought that such a small thing could become part of a story I would treasure?

I remember when your bag was stolen and I wanted to calm you down. I couldn't make everything disappear, but I wanted you to remember that Allah knows best, even when we don't understand what happened.

A letter from Muhammad · 2 of 3

The little things.

I notice the little things about you. How you encourage me to study, even when STA121 tries to humble me. 😂 How you remind me to eat, rest, and take care of myself. How you believe in me and motivate me without realizing how much your words mean.

I appreciate you more than I always know how to express. I don't want my love to be only sweet words. I want it to show in my patience, honesty, effort, respect, and the way I treat you when life is not easy.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. I am still growing, still learning, and still working towards the man I want to become. But I pray that I keep becoming someone who brings you peace rather than confusion, reassurance rather than fear, and kindness rather than unnecessary pain.

You don't have to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You can have difficult days, quiet days, and days when you need rest. Your health matters. Your peace matters. Your dreams matter. You matter beyond what you do for anyone else.

A letter from Muhammad · 3 of 3

My hope for us.

If Allah wills it for us, I pray that one day we will look back at these little beginnings from a home of our own. A home filled with sakinah, laughter, understanding, mercy, and barakah.

I pray for a love that helps us become better people. A love that does not make us forget our Creator, but brings us closer to Him. A love with patience, good character, sincere effort, and the intention to do things the right way.

May Allah protect the good in you, ease what feels heavy, bless your efforts, grant you beneficial knowledge, and place light in your path.

I don't know every detail of what the future holds. But I want to keep growing, working, praying, and doing my best. If Allah writes our future together, I pray that we meet it with grateful hearts.

You are precious to me, Sarauniya. I hope my actions continue to remind you of that, long after you've finished reading these words.

With love and du'a,
Muhammad 💜
